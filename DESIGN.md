# Whirlwind visual identity

Whirlwind is an engineering control center. Preserve navigation, execution controls, evidence, permission requests, and responsive layouts. The identity expresses velocity, control, and correctness without adding motion that distracts from operating tasks.

## Product language

Wordmark: **whirlwind_**. Signature: **ENGINEERING IN MOTION**.

Primary message: **Ideas in. Verified software out.**

Positioning: an open-source, agent-agnostic engineering harness. Bring your agents; Whirlwind handles the engineering process through autonomous execution loops.

Capabilities: agent-agnostic, ACP-native, durable execution, evidence-driven.

## Tokens

Source of truth: `web/app/globals.css`.

| Role | Color | Token |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Midnight canvas | `#0B1220` | `--paper` |
| Cloud white foreground | `#F8FAFC` | `--ink` |
| Ion teal actions and success | `#5EEAD4` | `--ion-teal` |
| Velocity blue information | `#38BDF8` | `--velocity-blue` |
| Energy violet evidence | `#A78BFA` | `--energy-violet` |
| Signal amber keyboard focus | `#FBBF24` | `--signal-amber` |

Existing `--cyan`, `--lime`, `--violet`, and `--focus` component tokens map to brand colors. Supporting dark surfaces and muted text retain readable contrast. Errors retain a separate red semantic color.

## Composition and behavior

Use a compact lower-case wordmark with teal underscore and a small signature in the navigation rail. The workspace introduces the product message, positioning, and capability list before live work. Task-specific screens retain direct operational headings.

Keep system sans-serif typography for the operational interface, balanced heading spacing, visible amber focus, teal carets, and themed selection and scrollbars. Sidebar becomes horizontal navigation below 700px. Capability labels wrap naturally. Honor reduced-motion preferences.
